The paper is a preprint dated 6 November 2026. It has not been submitted to arXiv
or a journal. Its model-free diagnostic contains 2,520 measured queries over
60 synthetic scenarios, plus 180 warmups, eleven operational controls, and
22 selected contract counterexamples replayed on the same frozen executable.
These measurements test bounded runtime behavior; they do not measure autonomous
patch correctness or real-repository issue-resolution success.

The public source builds the PDFs from their retained vector figures. Original
data, failed and recovery runs, and provenance receipts remain in the full research
and evidence archives. Earlier drafts in the parent paper directory document an
older experiment and are preserved separately.
